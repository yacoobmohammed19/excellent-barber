Our Story

Born in Algeria.
Perfected in Joburg.

Excellent Barber is a haven for those who value style, precision and a first-class grooming experience — founded over eight years ago by Algerian-born barber and entrepreneur Rafik Benrais.

What started as a single chair grew into two busy branches, anchored by the flagship in Sunward Park, Boksburg. The tools and the trends move on. The promise painted on the door never does — clean cut, close shave, excellent every time.
